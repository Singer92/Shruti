How to allow the microphone
  1. Tap the small icon at the left of the address bar (lock or sliders).
  2. Tap Permissions, then Microphone, and choose Allow.
  3. Refresh this page and tap Start again.

Opened this link from WhatsApp or Instagram? Tap the three dots at the top right and choose Open in Chrome (or Open in browser), then try again.

Still blocked? Phone Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Microphone must be allowed.

Tip: use earphones so the tanpura is not picked up by the microphone.
